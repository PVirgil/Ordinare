# Ordinare

**Everything in its place.**

Ordinare is an all-in-one personal organization platform designed to bring the different parts of your life into one clean, connected workspace.

Tasks, projects, events, notes, contacts, goals, bookmarks, and personal spaces can all live together instead of being scattered across separate apps.

The goal is simple: **make staying organized feel effortless.**

---

## ✨ What is Ordinare?

Most productivity tools are built around one specific thing.

A task manager manages tasks.
A calendar manages dates.
A notes app stores notes.
A contacts app stores people.
A project manager tracks projects.

Ordinare takes a different approach.

It acts as a **personal command center** where all of those things can coexist and eventually connect with one another.

Instead of asking:

> "Which app did I put that in?"

Ordinare is designed around a better question:

> **"What do I need to know or do?"**

---

## 🚀 Features

### ☀️ Today

Start each day with a clear overview of what matters.

The Today dashboard brings together:

* Open tasks
* Items due today
* Active projects
* Saved information
* Priorities
* Upcoming events
* Daily organization insights

It gives you one place to understand your day before deciding what to work on.

---

### 📥 Universal Inbox

Not everything needs to be perfectly organized the moment you think of it.

The Inbox provides a quick place to capture something first and decide where it belongs later.

Save an idea, responsibility, reminder, or piece of information without interrupting what you're doing.

---

### ✅ Tasks

Capture things you need to do and keep them connected to the areas of your life they belong to.

Tasks can include:

* Titles
* Details
* Spaces
* Dates
* Completion status

Completed tasks can be checked off while unfinished work remains visible in your priority queue.

---

### 📁 Projects

Projects give larger goals and multi-step work their own place.

Use them for things like:

* Planning a trip
* Building a website
* Renovating a room
* Launching a business
* Organizing an event
* Completing a school project

Projects can contain context, dates, progress, and connections to different areas of your life.

---

### 📅 Calendar

See everything in Ordinare that has a date attached to it.

The Calendar brings dated tasks, events, deadlines, and other time-sensitive information into a chronological view.

---

### 📚 Library

Your Library acts as a searchable personal knowledge collection.

Store things such as:

* Notes
* Bookmarks
* Ideas
* Reference information
* Useful links
* Things you want to remember

Instead of information disappearing into browser tabs or random documents, it can have a permanent home.

---

### 👥 People

Ordinare includes a lightweight personal relationship manager.

Create records for the people in your life and save:

* Name
* Phone number
* Email address
* Relationship
* Personal notes

Use it for friends, family, coworkers, clients, collaborators, or anyone else you want to remember.

Phone numbers and email addresses are directly accessible from each person's contact card.

---

### 🎯 Goals

Goals provide a home for the bigger outcomes you're working toward.

They are designed to eventually connect long-term ambitions with the projects and tasks that actually move them forward.

---

### 🗂️ Spaces

Spaces separate different areas of your life without forcing you to use completely different apps.

Examples include:

* Personal
* Work
* Home
* Finance
* School
* Business
* Travel
* Fitness
* Side projects

Spaces are customizable, allowing Ordinare to adapt to the way **you** organize your life.

---

## ✦ Command Bar

Ordinare includes a universal command and capture interface.

Instead of navigating through menus, you can quickly enter something you want to remember or do.

Examples:

```text
Remind me to renew my passport Friday
```

```text
Add project: Plan Japan trip
```

```text
Save note: Call the electrician about the kitchen lights
```

The command interface is designed to become one of the fastest ways to interact with Ordinare.

---

## 🌙 Light & Dark Mode

Ordinare supports both light and dark interfaces.

The UI automatically maintains appropriate contrast, form controls, text behavior, and interaction states across both themes.

---

## 📱 Responsive Design

Ordinare is designed for:

**Desktop · Laptop · Tablet · Mobile**

The interface adapts across screen sizes with responsive navigation, layouts, cards, forms, and controls.

On smaller devices, the desktop navigation becomes a mobile sidebar while the primary workspace remains focused on the content you're currently using.

---

## 🔒 Local-First

The current version of Ordinare is local-first.

User-created information is stored directly in the browser using `localStorage`.

This means the application can retain your workspace between sessions without requiring an account or external database.

> Clearing browser storage may remove locally stored Ordinare data.

Cloud accounts, synchronization, and persistent server-side storage are natural future extensions of the platform.

---

## 🧠 The Vision

Ordinare isn't intended to become another productivity app with dozens of disconnected features.

The long-term vision is to create an **organization layer for your entire life**.

A project shouldn't exist separately from its tasks.

A meeting shouldn't exist separately from the person attending it.

A trip shouldn't exist separately from its reservations, dates, notes, documents, and reminders.

A goal shouldn't exist separately from the work required to accomplish it.

Everything should be able to connect.

That is the idea behind Ordinare.

* **HTML5**
* **CSS3**

This keeps the application fast, understandable, and easy to iterate on.

## 🤖 Future AI Organization

One of Ordinare's biggest opportunities is reducing how much manual organization the user has to perform.

Instead of requiring users to constantly choose folders, categories, dates, projects, and tags, future versions could allow Ordinare to understand what an item is and suggest where it belongs.

For example:

```text
Book hotel for Italy trip before October 12
```

could eventually be understood as:

**Type:** Task
**Project:** Italy Trip
**Category:** Travel
**Deadline:** October 12

The user captures the thought.

**Ordinare handles the organization.**

## 🎨 Design Philosophy

Ordinare is built around several principles:

**Clarity over complexity.**
Important information should be understandable immediately.

**Capture first, organize second.**
Saving something shouldn't require stopping to categorize it perfectly.

**Everything can be connected.**
Tasks, people, projects, events, goals, and information shouldn't exist in isolation.

**Your system should adapt to you.**
Users shouldn't have to restructure their lives around the limitations of their productivity software.

**Organization should reduce work, not create more of it.**
